# J1 Code licensing boundaries

New protected J1 contributions introduced by the October 5, 2026 licensing-change
commit and later revisions are subject to [LICENSE](LICENSE). You may inspect
public source and use unmodified official builds for your own work. Reusing new
protected J1 material in another product, modifying it, or redistributing it
requires separate permission. The terms do not restrict output you create with J1.

This is a source-available project with mixed licensing. It is not exclusively
proprietary and the new material is not generally open source. Public access
does not provide a general reuse license for that new material.

## Material that remains MIT or separately licensed

- T3 Code and its inherited portions retain MIT, including its full copyright and
  permission notice preserved in [licenses/T3-MIT.txt](licenses/T3-MIT.txt).
- Previously MIT-distributed J1 material through commit
  `57ade75c25aff71b8345795b4d4d2261a843f62a`, earlier releases, and unchanged copies
  of that material retain MIT permissions. This change does not revoke them.
- Dependencies, vendored assets, and explicitly licensed components retain their
  own notices and permissions. This policy does not override those licenses.

For a mixed file, new protected expressions and older MIT portions have different
permissions. Moving or renaming older code does not make it proprietary. Compare
against the prior baseline and preserve the component notices; do not assume
every line of a changed file has become exclusively owned by J1's maintainer.

## Distribution and contributions

Official-build permission covers installation, use, and necessary backup copies;
it does not grant general redistribution rights to new protected material. MIT
and other component permissions remain independent. Existing downloaded releases
keep the terms they were originally distributed under.

The build notice manifest includes the J1 terms and the full T3/prior MIT notice
alongside dependency notices for web, desktop, and mobile. Server packages ship a
copy of the current terms. Public downloads and update URLs are unchanged.

New contributions require rights clearance and an agreed contribution license
before acceptance. A contribution does not automatically transfer copyright.

Copyright eligibility and ownership depend on actual authorship, including the
human contribution to AI-assisted work. This policy is not an ownership audit or
a guarantee against copying. Obtain an IP lawyer's review before commercial
enforcement or rights-sensitive distribution.
